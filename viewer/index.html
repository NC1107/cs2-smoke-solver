      How close to the target (horizontally) a throw must land for the solver to keep it at all - enforced against each throw's exact simulated landing. Unlike the Precision display filter, this changes what gets computed.

      How far around a clicked throw spot to look for stand positions. "Exact spot" searches only where you clicked, with the fine angle scan and the verify gate opened up, so it finds a throw from that precise position even when it is an awkward one - use it when you know where you want to stand.

      Candidates below this stability are discarded during solving, before you ever see them. Lower it to surface touchy-but-real lineups.

      Halves the aim-angle step the sweep samples (0.5° near a click). Finds lineups whose working angle window the normal scan steps over. Roughly 3x solve time.

      Whether smokes may fly through breakable glass (e.g. Nuke's roof panes over Heaven) and open doorways. Round start assumes everything intact and closed - pick a broken state for mid-round lineups that only exist once the glass is shot out or the door stands open.

      Gives a lineup credit for being thrown from a spot pros use in real matches. Maps with thousands of demo points can otherwise crowd out spots that are simply better throws, so turn this off to rank on the throw alone.
